BEFORE YOU TAKE THE OATH

Good to know.

Can I place an order yet?

Not yet. This is our live test storefront. You can explore the concepts, select a size and try the bag, but checkout is disabled and no payments or orders are taken.

Are these the final designs?

These are illustrative concepts for the first collection. Final artwork, garment photographs, available colours and prices will be added once approved.

What about sizing and delivery?

Garment-specific measurements, materials, delivery destinations, costs and return information will be published before orders open. The size choices in this preview are for testing only.

What does this preview save?

Your test bag is stored only in this browser so you can return to it. We don't collect your name, email or payment details in this version. You can clear the bag at any time.
